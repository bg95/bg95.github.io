AIAA3072 — Week 11: Quantum Error Correction Lab

1. Extract the entire ZIP into a folder.
2. Open index.html in a modern web browser.
3. Start with “Redundancy & syndromes” and follow the seven sections.

Keep all the extracted files together. No installation, server, account,
or internet connection is needed. This package contains only the interactive
webpage and its assets; lecture-note PDFs and source files are not included.

Click numbered markers directly on each circuit to inspect numerical states.
Tab/Enter and left/right arrow keys also navigate the circuit. The complete
correction circuit includes encoding, error, ancilla checks, and recovery.

Click a qubit to cycle I -> X -> Y -> Z -> I. The error gates in Experiment 02
are clickable too. In Experiment 03, the error painter and the qubits beside
the recovery circuit edit the same errors. Changing an error keeps your selected time step and updates its state.
Click the recovery steps to apply the bit and phase gates.

Experiment 04 introduces basic stabilizer checks. In Experiment 05, prepare
the coherent state, measure its syndrome, then correct the surviving branch.
Prepare again to compare outcomes. Experiment 06 develops dimension and recovery.

Try changing the input, painting X/Y/Z errors, and answering the short checks. On a phone, wide circuits
and tables scroll sideways within their panels.

Your exploration progress stays in this browser. “Reset progress” clears it.
Quiz answers and simulated measurements are not sent anywhere.

This is a teaching model with ideal encoding, checks, and recovery.
Week 12 introduces noisy checks and fault tolerance.

The desktop layout uses a comfortable 90% scale. Phone text keeps its original size.
